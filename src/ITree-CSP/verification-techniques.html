How you prove things in this repository

Every technique sits on one operational LTS over the coinductive PTree. Above it are two families — equivalences (prove two processes equal) and refinements (prove behaviour-set inclusion) — linked by a soundness ladder, plus property layers for deadlock/liveness, LTL, and priority. The recurring method: prove a property on a small spec, then transfer it to the real system along ≈DR.

◆ The method in three moves

1 · Specify

Prove on a small spec

Show the property (deadlock-freedom, an FD equation, an LTL formula) on an abstract, tractable process — e.g. CopySpec.

→

2 · Transfer

Carry it along ≈DR

A divergence-respecting weak bisimulation between spec and implementation transfers the property verbatim via a transfer theorem.

→

3 · Compose

Scale with the laws

Congruence + monotonicity (Par/Hide/…) assemble whole-system proofs from component proofs.

◆ The soundness ladder & transfer hub

≈DR is the hub: strong bisim and expansion feed into it, it projects down to the failures-divergences model, and it is the relation along which every property transfers. Arrow labels are the actual theorems.

Each relation/technique, its home module, and what it lets you prove. Symbols are the actual Agda names.
